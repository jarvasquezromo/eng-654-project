Pairwise differences

Sample configurations uniformly inside the joint limits and record the worst position and orientation disagreement for each pair of models.

The supplied solver's example (pose.json: the zero-joint tool pose) returns 8 configurations. Each one is pushed through our PoE and D–H models and compared with the requested pose. This also confirms that our tool0 frame and quaternion convention \([w,x,y,z]\) match the solver.

Standard D–H

\({}^0T_6 = \prod_{i=1}^6 R_z(q_i+\theta_{i,0})\,T_z(d_i)\,T_x(a_i)\,R_x(\alpha_i)\) with the table from Step 1. It shares no code with the URDF path.

Product of exponentials

\(T = e^{[S_1]q_1}\cdots e^{[S_6]q_6}M\), with each exponential from the closed-form \(SE(3)\) Rodrigues formula.

Error measures

Position \(\|p_A-p_B\|\). Orientation \(\angle(R_A^\top R_B)\) computed with \(\operatorname{atan2}\) so it stays accurate near zero (an \(\arccos\) formula bottoms out at about \(10^{-8}\) rad).
