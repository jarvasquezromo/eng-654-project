Step 4 · Singularity in task space

Conditioning on a Cartesian plane

Pick a plane and its offset, fix the tool, then solve IK at every point and evaluate the full Jacobian of each solution.

Plane

Tool orientation (tool z axis = approach)

Orientation = Rz(yaw) Ry(pitch) Rx(roll), held constant over the whole plane.

Tool length (TCP offset along tool z)

0 = flange (tool0). The map point is the TCP; the Jacobian is taken about the TCP.

Map

Tap (or drag) on the map. Every IK solution at that point is listed; pick one to pose the robot.

What each pixel is

A TCP position on the chosen plane with the fixed tool orientation. The flange pose is \(T_{0,\text{tool0}} = T_{\text{TCP}}\,\operatorname{Trans}(0,0,-\ell)\). All IK solutions are computed, then for each one the 6×6 geometric Jacobian about the TCP, with linear rows scaled by \(1/L\). The map shows \(1/\kappa = \sigma_{\min}/\sigma_{\max}\) of the best branch, of one chosen branch, or how many valid solutions exist. Grey means no valid solution.

IK in the browser

For a fixed \(q_6\), the frame-4 origin \(O_4 = p - d_6 z_6 - a_5 x_5(q_6)\) and the axis-5 direction \(z_4 = x_5 \times z_6\) are known. \(O_4\) depends only on \((q_1,q_2,q_3)\), giving 2 shoulder × 2 elbow closed-form arm solutions. The remaining condition, axis 5 ⟂ forearm axis, \(f(q_6) = z_3 \cdot z_4 = 0\), is solved per arm branch by sampling \(q_6\) and refining each sign change. \(q_4, q_5\) follow from \(z_4\) and \(x_5\). Every candidate is accepted only if our FK reproduces the pose to \(10^{-7}\).

Validation

On 400 random poses the JS solver and the supplied crb15000-ik agreed on 2947 configurations, with 3 found only by the supplied solver (all at the elbow reach boundary) and none found only by the JS one. node tests/check.mjs repeats the example and a 1000-pose round trip.

Branch labels

Front/back: whether \(q_1\) points at \(O_4\) or past it. \(q_3 \gtrless q_3^*\): which side of the elbow singularity (\(q_3^* = -76.83^\circ\)). \(q_5 \gtrless 0\): wrist flip. These are pointwise labels, good for reading the map; persistent branch tracking is Step 5.
