Tool frame

link_6 → flange is identity and flange → tool0 is a pure rotation \(R_y(\pi/2)\). So tool0's z-axis points along joint 6's axis and its x-axis along −z of the base at home.

What the axes say about the geometry

Axes 2 and 3 are parallel (both \(y\)); axes 4 and 6 are parallel at home but 0.080 m apart; axes 4 and 5 intersect. The wrist is therefore not spherical: axis 6 misses the intersection of axes 4 and 5. This offset is why the course's clean shoulder × elbow × wrist factorisation does not carry over directly.

Standard convention \(\;{}^{i-1}T_i = R_z(\theta_i)\,T_z(d_i)\,T_x(a_i)\,R_x(\alpha_i)\), with \(\theta_i = q_i + \theta_{i,0}\). Each \(z_{i-1}\) is chosen in the same direction as the URDF axis of joint \(i\), so joint signs match the URDF and the supplied IK solver.
