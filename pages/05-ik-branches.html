Validate and organise into persistent branches

All candidates along the contour of the current placement (Step 6), checked with our FK, periodic angles resolved, limits applied, then linked sample to sample into branches.

Persistent branches (tap one)

Conventions applied to every candidate

q₁, q₂, q₄, q₅ are wrapped to (−180°, 180°]; their range is exactly one turn, so the representative is unique and crossing ±180° is a real discontinuity. q₃ is shifted by whole turns into [−225°, 85°]; if no shift fits, the candidate violates the limit. q₆ has a 540° range, so a candidate can have two physical representatives (q₆ and q₆ ± 360°); both are kept for the continuity search.

The example output of the supplied crb15000-ik (pose p = (0.571, 0, 0.899) m, q = [0.7071, 0, 0.7071, 0]), re-checked with our own PoE FK.

Candidates

At every contour sample the browser IK (same structure as the supplied solver: sweep of q₆, closed-form arm, root refinement) returns every configuration. A candidate is kept only if our FK reproduces the pose to 1e-7 m / rad.

Persistent branches

Between consecutive samples, candidates are matched greedily by the smallest torus distance \(\max_i |\mathrm{wrap}(q_i^{k+1} - q_i^{k})|\), with a gate of 1.5 Δqmax. A branch ends when nothing is within the gate (two branches meet at a singularity, or the pose leaves the reachable set); unmatched candidates start new branches. Labels (shoulder / elbow side / wrist sign) can change along a branch, which is why tracking is done on q itself.

Closing the loop

The contour is closed, so the last sample is matched back to the first. A branch can return to itself, or to a different branch (it permutes, like going around a branch point), which matters for repeating the task.
